## 核心FE
- Item: 被描述其标量属性的实体，如 "The light is bright enough"
- Scale: 被描述的 Item 属性维度，如 "sufficient in number and proficiency"
- Enabled_situation: 判断临界值的赋能情境，如 "enough for thirty people", "too heavy for Him to lift"

## 外围FE
- Degree: 修饰程度，如 "almost powerful enough"

## 主题外功能FE
